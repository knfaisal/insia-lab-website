Our research

The INSIA Lab studies how to extract reliable information from complex, time-varying signals. We combine the mathematics of signal processing (time–frequency analysis and adaptive decomposition) with modern machine learning, and apply it wherever signals are noisy, non-stationary and important: radar, wearables, the heart, the brain and the human hand.

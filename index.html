About the lab

The Intelligent Next-gen Signal & Image Analysis (INSIA) Lab at the Military Institute of Science and Technology studies how to extract reliable information from complex, time-varying signals.

We pair the mathematics of signal processing with modern machine learning to build sensing systems that are accurate, interpretable and efficient enough to deploy.

Meet the team → The director
Why we exist

Bangladesh has no shortage of talented students, but few places where they can do serious research. Facilities are limited, experienced researchers are few and stretched thin, and a day-to-day research-lab culture has yet to take root. INSIA was started to change that.

Our vision

A Bangladesh where world-class research is done at home, and where scientists trained here help shape their fields worldwide.

Our mission

To build the research culture Bangladesh is missing: a friendly lab where students work side by side with experienced researchers, grow from the fundamentals to the frontier, and publish work that matters in the real world.

  1. 01

    Mentorship up close

    Experienced researchers sit down with every student, one to one. Questions are welcome, doors stay open, and nobody is left to figure research out alone.

  2. 02

    From basics to frontier

    Every member starts with the fundamentals (signals, mathematics, programming, reading papers) and moves step by step to advanced, original research.

  3. 03

    Work that matters

    We cover the full chain: foundational theory, translational methods that carry theory into practice, and applied systems that work outside the lab.

  4. 04

    Scientists for Bangladesh and the world

    We train research scientists who can compete anywhere, and who bring that impact back home.
